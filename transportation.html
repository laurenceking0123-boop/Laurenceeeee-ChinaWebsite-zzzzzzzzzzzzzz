Transportation Guide

Navigate China's modern transit networks seamlessly with our comprehensive travel options.

Intercity

High-Speed Train (CRH)

Connects major Chinese economic hubs like Beijing, Shanghai, and Guangzhou at speeds reaching up to 350 km/h. Smooth, rapid, and highly reliable.

Intracity

Metro Transit Systems

Extensive and modern subway routes spanning throughout city networks. Fully accessible with english signage prompts and contactless mobile payment turnstiles.

🚗
On-Demand

DiDi Ride-Hailing

China's primary localized ride service alternative to Uber. Features integrated real-time text translations for seamless communications between international riders and local drivers.

Express Link

Shanghai Maglev

The world's fastest commercial high-speed magnetic levitation line. Bridges Shanghai Pudong Airport straight to the urban metro terminal core links in under 8 minutes.

Localized

City Bus Networks

Affordable, extensive transit alternative that blankets all major districts. Easily navigated via smartphone mapping integrations across modern bus fleets.

Shared Urban Bicycles

Convenient dockless bicycle rentals scattered across sidewalks. Accessible instantly by scanning barcode labels via app integrations like Alipay or WeChat Pay.
