Great Wall

Beijing

Beijing is the historic and modern capital of China, known for ancient palaces, advanced technology, massive shopping districts, and vibrant culture.

Shanghai

Shanghai is famous for its stunning waterfront skyline along the Bund, historical gardens, and a beautiful blend of Eastern and Western traditions.

Jiuzhaigou Valley

Jiuzhaigou National Park is a network of valleys in China Sichuan province. In the northern Shuzheng Valley, Nuorilang Waterfall cascades from the edge of a large tree-fringed lake.

Forbidden City

Forbidden City represent the political and historical heart of Beijing. It was the residence of 24 Ming and Qing dynasty Emperors and it's the imperial palace complex.

The West Lake

West Lake, also called Xihu, is a freshwater lake in Hangzhou. Located west of the city’s former walled area, it covers 6.39 km² and measures about 3.2 km north to south and 2.8 km east to west.
